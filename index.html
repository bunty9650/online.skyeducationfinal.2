Which courses are available through the portal?

Students can explore programs such as Online MBA, MCA, M.Com, MA, BBA, BCA, B.Com, and BA along with various specializations.

Are the universities listed on the portal UGC approved?

Yes. The portal features universities that hold relevant approvals and recognitions such as UGC entitlement and other regulatory accreditations.

Is an Online Degree valid for government jobs?

Yes. Degrees earned from recognized and UGC-entitled universities are generally valid for higher education, private jobs, and government job opportunities as per prevailing regulations.

Can I pursue an Online MBA while working full-time?

Yes. Online MBA programs are designed for working professionals, entrepreneurs, and graduates who wish to continue their education without leaving their jobs.

What MBA specializations are available?

Students can choose from specializations such as Marketing, Finance, Human Resource Management, Business Analytics, Digital Marketing, Operations, Healthcare Management, Logistics & Supply Chain Management, and more.

How can I select the right university for my course?

The portal allows you to compare universities based on fees, approvals, rankings, location, specializations, learning support, and career objectives.

Is EMI facility available for Online Degree programs?

Yes. Many universities featured on the portal offer EMI and flexible payment options to make higher education more affordable.

What documents are required for admission?

Generally, students need educational certificates, identity proof, passport-size photographs, and other university-specific documents during the admission process.

Will I get support during the admission process?

Yes. Dedicated counselors assist students with university selection, registration, documentation, admission, and onboarding.

How are classes conducted in Online Degree programs?

Classes are conducted through a Learning Management System (LMS) that provides access to recorded lectures, study material, assignments, and live sessions.

Can I access study material from anywhere in India?

Yes. Online and Distance learning programs allow students to access learning resources from any location with internet connectivity.

Do universities provide placement assistance?

Many universities offer career services, placement support, resume-building guidance, and interview preparation assistance to eligible students.

Is there any counseling fee for course guidance?

Typically, students can receive course and university counseling to help them choose the most suitable program based on their educational and career goals.

Can graduates from any stream apply for Online Master's programs?

Eligibility depends on the chosen program and university requirements. Most master's programs require a recognized bachelor's degree from an approved institution.

How can I apply through Sky Education Group?

Students can submit their details through the portal, connect with an expert counselor, compare universities, complete documentation, and proceed with admission.
